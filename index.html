    How this model was made

    Labels recovered from the Visage review video (1 frame per 0.6 mm slice, registered to the DICOM at r≈0.998). Overlay colour per pixel was fitted against the CT render; pieces were linked through z without ever merging pieces Visage outlined apart, and each 3D piece was colour-checked as a whole. Surfaces: Gaussian (0.45 mm z, 0.15 mm in-plane), marching cubes, Taubin smoothing. Overview 0.40 mm; sections load 0.22 mm. Views are in scanner orientation (no Frankfort landmarks in this field of view).
